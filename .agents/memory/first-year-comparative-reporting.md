---
name: First-year comparative reporting
description: Reporting behavior when an entity has no prior-period ledger activity
---

For a first reporting year, missing prior-period ledger activity must remain a visible comparative-information warning but must not block report finalization when the other required controls pass.

**Why:** A newly formed company may have no legitimate comparative transactions; requiring fabricated prior-period journals would be worse than presenting a controlled current-period-only report.

**How to apply:** Keep comparative columns hidden when no prior-period evidence is available unless the reviewer enables them, explain the limitation in the report review, and reserve blocking status for substantive accounting controls or explicit required inputs.
